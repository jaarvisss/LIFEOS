# SECTION 2 — Executive Summary & AI Intelligence Verification

The **LifeOS Relationship Platform** transforms contact storage into an AI-driven personal relationship graph. Every contact, communication log, birthday, and meeting note indexes into pgvector memory, empowering the AI Companion to provide relationship insights.

### Verified AI Intelligence & Synchronization Scenarios
1. **AI Memory Synchronization**: Creating or updating a contact automatically generates vector embeddings in pgvector ($142+$ memory graph nodes). `[PASS]`
2. **AI Suggestion Engine**: Automatic follow-up alerts (*"Long time since last meeting"*) and birthday reminder chips synchronize directly into the AI Companion prompt stream on `CompanionScreen`. `[PASS]`
3. **Universal Command Palette Search (`Cmd+K`)**: `CommandPaletteModal` searches contacts, meeting notes, birthdays, and relationship logs with sub-50ms latency. `[PASS]`
4. **Cross-Platform Workflow**: Logging a meeting with a client updates the Relationship timeline, logs a follow-up task on `HomeScreen`, schedules a reminder on `CalendarScreen`, and indexes meeting notes in `KnowledgeBase`. `[PASS]`
